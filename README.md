# J.A. SOVEREIGN GOD LIMITED - Official Website Project
Heavy Construction Equipment & Construction Solutions
Website: www.jasovereigngod.com
Location: Tema, Greater Accra, Ghana.

## Overview
The official website for J.A. Sovereign God Limited, specializing in heavy construction machinery rental, equipment sales, servicing, repairs, preventive maintenance, civil earthmoving construction operations, and professional heavy equipment operator training in Tema, Ghana.

## Technology Stack
- **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible markup across all 11 official website pages.
- **CSS3**: Preserved modern industrial layout architecture with CSS Grid, Flexbox, custom design tokens, and smooth transitions. Styled in the official brand palette: Vibrant Safety Orange (`#FF662C`), Dark Charcoal (`#303030`), Deep Surface (`#242424`), and Clean White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Zero-dependency client-side scripts managing:
  - Direct WhatsApp enquiry and quote forms: Professionally formatted WhatsApp dispatch to primary business line `0244 406 085` (`+233 244 406 085`).
  - Heavy equipment operator training registration forms with input validation and instant formatted WhatsApp dispatch.
  - Interactive FAQ collapsible accordions.
  - Responsive mobile drawer navigation with animated submenus.
  - Animated quality metrics and stat counters.
  - Quick quotation and equipment consultation modal dialogs.
  - Direct deep-linking and service pre-selection via URL query parameters.

## Website Pages
1. `index.html` - Homepage (Hero, Three-Image Collage with Rotating Badge, Equipment Highlights, Operator Training Spotlight, Direct WhatsApp Consultation Modal, Client Trust Metrics)
2. `about.html` - About Us (Company Profile, Vision, Mission, Core Values, Leadership & Governance)
3. `services.html` - Services (Machinery Rental, Equipment Sales, Servicing & Repairs, Construction Operations, Operator Training)
4. `equipment.html` - Heavy Machinery Fleet (Backhoes, Excavators, Crawler Tractors, Power Shovels, Rollers, Bulldozers, Wheel Tractors, Graders, Rippers)
5. `training.html` - Heavy Equipment Operator Training Centre (Course modules, hands-on field training, full registration form with WhatsApp dispatch)
6. `contact.html` - Contact Us (Direct WhatsApp enquiry form, location details, direct call & WhatsApp links)
7. `faq.html` - Frequently Asked Questions (Comprehensive Q&A covering rentals, sales, maintenance, safety, and certifications)
8. `sitemap.html` - Sitemap (Structured index of all sections, categories, and policies)
9. `privacy.html` - Privacy Policy (Data protection and customer confidentiality)
10. `terms.html` - Terms and Conditions (Machinery rental and operational terms)
11. `accessibility.html` - Accessibility Statement (Commitment to inclusive web standards)

## How to Run & Build
- Development server: `npm run dev` (Runs Vite on port 3000)
- Production build: `npm run build`
- TypeScript check: `npm run lint`
